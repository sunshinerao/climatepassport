# GEO Entity Consistency Audit - 2026-07-12

## 需求解读

Review whether the public multilingual entity pages consistently communicate the core Climate Passport entity relationships for SEO/GEO and AI interpretation: what Climate Passport is, what Climate Passport ID is, how credentials/records/verification relate, and what the product is not.

## 修改方法

Audited the source content for the stable sitemap entity pages: About, Climate Passport ID, Verifiable Credentials, Climate Records and Credentials, Certificates, FAQ, Privacy and Terms. The audit focused on entity definition, identity boundary, credential/record relationship, verification semantics, language availability and internal linking.

## 修改内容

- Confirmed canonical URL and hreflang architecture are consistent across current sitemap pages after the production audit.
- Confirmed About and Climate Passport ID pages clearly state that Climate Passport is not a government-issued identity, national identity credential or travel document.
- Confirmed Climate Records and Credentials explains climate credentials, verification, learning records, participation records, action records and climate digital identity across en/zh/fr/de.
- Confirmed Verifiable Credentials explains credentials as records connected to learning, participation, certificates, achievements and action history.
- Fixed the About page language-content gap: About now provides first-class English, Chinese, French and German entity copy instead of using English fallback content for French and German.
- Fixed a minor linking gap: the About page now links to the localized Climate Records and Credentials guide for en/zh/fr/de instead of pointing non-English readers to the English guide.
- Strengthened the identity boundary across Verifiable Credentials and Certificate Verification pages in all supported languages, clarifying that credentials and certificates are platform records, not government identities, national identity credentials or travel documents.

## Audit Outcome

The core entity model is now coherent and consistent across the public entity surfaces reviewed: About, Climate Passport ID, Verifiable Credentials, Certificate Verification and Climate Records and Credentials. English, Chinese, French and German pages now have first-class locale-specific body copy for these entity topics instead of relying on English fallback content.
