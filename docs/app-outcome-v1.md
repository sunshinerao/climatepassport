## Receipts, ordering and retry

Canonical digest is SHA-256 of recursively key-sorted compact JSON of the entire validated envelope (arrays retain order). stream = JSON.stringify([sourceApp,sourceAuthority,issuer,subject,sourceRecord.id]). Response:
`{schemaVersion:"app-outcome/1",applied,state,eventId,digest,sourceRecord:{id,version},stream,remoteRecordRef,currentVersion,terminalRevoked}`.
200+applied true means this particular immutable event was applied historically. currentVersion may already be greater after gap draining; terminalRevoked reports current archive state. This is not public visibility or qualification. Exact replay/reconcile returns the same event binding. No private evidence is returned in receipts. 202+applied false/state gap_pending is received only, never synced. Conflicting ID/digest or different event for the same version =>409. Stale =>409. Missing mapping/authority/consent =>403. Unknown receipt =>404. 503 means unavailable; source retains identical ID/digest, bounded backoff and operator attention.

New record uses upsert/version1; thereafter correct/revoke increments by one and cites the current accepted head. Gaps are bounded to 100 ahead, persisted and automatically drained when predecessors arrive, with live consent/prior revalidation. Invalid pending transitions permanently quarantine the stream: all buffered events become BLOCKED and private projection/evidence is erased immediately. Exact retry/reconcile of a BLOCKED event returns HTTP409 with applied:false/state:blocked and its exact binding; it is permanent operator attention, never retry_wait/gap_pending. Further correct/revoke at a reserved or later version returns409 OUTCOME_BLOCKED. No automatic version skipping, event rewriting or new-ID repair exists. Source business review must terminate that export and, if a legitimate new outcome is needed, create a NEW business record plus separately approved mapping and fresh consent. CP consent withdrawal also remains available; the quarantined projection is already suppressed. Minimal tombstone purge applies after the configured interval. Revocation/withdrawal is terminal; reopening requires a NEW business record and separately approved binding/consent. Ambiguous delivery uses reconcile or exact retry, never a new recovery event ID. No snapshot shortcut. Source outbox creation must be atomic with local authoritative completion/eligibility; CP never reads the source business DB.

## Owner retention and deletion rule

Withdraw/supersede consent in CP erases projection kind/status/evidence and all buffered payloads atomically, immediately suppresses owner reads, and preserves only source-owned IDs, hashes, versions, operation/time/status and opaque record reference. A minimal source revoke remains admissible for the known record. Accepted payloads are erased after application (the live private projection is the only evidence-reference copy). Pending content has a maximum 30-day retention.

The synthetic fixture default is 30 days; CP_APP_OUTCOME_TOMBSTONE_DAYS configures 1–365 days. Production retention is not approved or deployed. Terminal anti-replay tombstones expire after the configured interval after first revoke/withdrawal/supersession. The operator-owned DAILY purge removes the exact record binding, stream and inbox/history by FK cascade; missing binding then rejects late delivery. CP account deletion or source deletion also cascades all bindings/history. Audit retains only opaque CP stream UUID/action/result/time, no subject, evidence, external record ID or digest. After purge, trusted provisioning MUST NOT rebind that old business record; a new authorized outcome requires a new source business record ID and fresh current receipt. No public provisioning route exists. The daily purge must be enabled and verified before any real machine source can be activated; its operational approval is included in the activation package, not inferred here.
