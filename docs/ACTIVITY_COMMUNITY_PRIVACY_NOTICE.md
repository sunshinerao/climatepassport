# Activity Community Privacy Notice (Phase 4)

Activity Community is a bounded, optional space attached to one eligible PROJECT or CHALLENGE. It is not a public feed, directory, social graph, direct-message service, upload area, or search surface.

Only a participating user (or an authorized activity moderator) can access a community. Names and submitted text are visible only within that activity according to moderation state. Participant posts and comments are PREMODERATED; organizers, admins, and explicitly assigned community moderators may publish immediately and moderate content. Event-manager role alone does not grant community moderation rights.

Do not submit HTML, email addresses, phone numbers, payment details, government identifiers, or other personal contact information. The service rejects HTML and common contact-information patterns. Audit records and notification metadata contain identifiers, activity references, and outcomes only—not community content bodies.

Reports are deduplicated per reporter and content item. Content is retained only for the activity's operational and safety needs, subject to applicable platform retention procedures.
